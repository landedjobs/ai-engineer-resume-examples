# Bullet bank: leadership

22 bullets for senior, staff and lead roles, with or without a manager title. Leadership bullets need outcomes too. "Mentored engineers" says nothing; "mentored 4 engineers, 2 promoted" says something. Replace every `[placeholder]` with your own numbers and nouns.

Placeholders: `[N]` a count, `[X%]` a percentage, `[A]`/`[B]` before and after values, `[$N]` a dollar amount.

1. Led [N] engineers through [project], shipping in [N] weeks and raising [metric] from [A] to [B].
2. Set the architecture for [platform], now used by [N] teams for [N] features.
3. Wrote the AI release standard (golden sets, red-team sets, review rates by risk tier) adopted as policy by [org].
4. Grew the team from [A] to [B] engineers; hired [N] of them and designed the AI engineer interview loop.
5. Mentored [N] engineers into owning AI services; [N] were promoted within [period].
6. Led the incident review after [incident] and shipped the fix ([mechanism]), with zero repeats across [N] [requests / answers] since.
7. Decided against [building X / fine-tuning / a vendor] after a [N]-week study; the memo became the reference for [build-vs-buy] decisions.
8. Set the model strategy across [N] providers and [N] self-hosted models, cutting spend by [$N] a year at equal quality.
9. Ran the AI working group across [N] teams, cutting duplicate work and moving [N] teams onto shared [retrieval / eval / gateway] infrastructure.
10. Taught [N] internal workshops on [RAG / evals / agents]; [N] teams shipped features from the material within a quarter.
11. Led the migration from [old system] to [new system] across [N] services with no customer-facing downtime.
12. Partnered with [legal / security / compliance] to define rules for [AI data use / model providers], unblocking [N] launches.
13. Represented engineering in [N] enterprise customer reviews, including presenting eval results to [risk committees / CISOs].
14. Wrote the [N]-quarter technical roadmap for [area], with [X%] of committed items shipped on time.
15. Built the on-call rotation and runbooks for AI services, cutting mean time to recovery from [A] to [B].
16. Ran design reviews for [N] AI projects a quarter, catching [N] issues ([cost, privacy, eval gaps]) before build.
17. Spoke at [conference / meetup] on [topic]; the talk led to [N] inbound candidates and [N] hires.
18. Owned the AI budget of [$N] a year, reporting cost per feature monthly to leadership.
19. Coached [N] product managers on writing AI specs with eval criteria, used for every AI launch since [date].
20. Built the hiring rubric for AI engineers, raising onsite-to-offer rate from [A]% to [B]% with no drop in 6-month performance ratings.
