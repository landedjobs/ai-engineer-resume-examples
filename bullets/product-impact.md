# Bullet bank: product impact

23 bullets that connect AI work to users and the business. Use one or two of these per role; they are often the bullets that get you the interview. Replace every `[placeholder]` with your own numbers and nouns.

Placeholders: `[N]` a count, `[X%]` a percentage, `[A]`/`[B]` before and after values, `[$N]` a dollar amount.

1. Shipped [AI feature] used weekly by [X%] of active accounts within [N] months of launch.
2. Deflected [X%] of [support tickets / HR requests / IT tickets] with [assistant], saving an estimated [N] hours a month.
3. Cut [task] time for [users] from [A] to [B] across [N] [cases / documents] a month.
4. Raised [conversion / activation / retention] by [X%] in an A/B test of [AI feature] on [N]% of traffic.
5. Built the AI feature that became the top reason cited in [upgrade / renewal] surveys ([X%] of responses).
6. Moved [AI feature] from the [top] plan into the [mid] plan by cutting its cost per use [X%], adding [$N] in annual recurring revenue.
7. Took [feature] from prototype to GA in [N] weeks with [N] engineers.
8. Grew [AI product] from [A] to [B] weekly active users in [period].
9. Raised user satisfaction for [assistant] from [A] to [B] (out of 5) over [N]K ratings by fixing the top [N] failure types.
10. Reduced manual review for [process] from [A]% to [B]% of cases, saving the customer [N] FTE.
11. Ran [N] user interviews and session reviews that changed the [feature] design from [A] to [B]; usage rose [X%] after the change.
12. Launched [feature] in [N] languages, with per-language evals, raising usage outside [region] by [X%].
13. Built the onboarding flow for [AI product], raising first-week activation from [A]% to [B]%.
14. Shipped [N] customer-requested AI features in [period], each tied to a renewal or expansion worth [$N] in total.
15. Cut time to first useful answer for new users from [A] to [B] with suggested questions and better empty states.
16. Built the feedback loop (ratings, edits, flags) behind [feature]; [N]K signals a month now feed the eval set.
17. Replaced a [manual / rules-based] process with [AI system], handling [N] cases a [day / week] with [X%] accuracy and [N] fewer escalations.
18. Partnered with [sales / success] on [N] enterprise deals, building the demos and evals that closed [$N] in new contracts.
19. Shipped an internal AI tool for [team] used by [N] of [N] people daily, cutting [task] from [A] to [B].
20. Wrote the public launch post and docs for [feature]; [N] developers adopted the API in the first month.
