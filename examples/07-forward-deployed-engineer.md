# Example 07: Forward-deployed engineer (AI)

> **Fictional person.** Nadia Haddad, her employers, customers and numbers are invented for this example. Any resemblance to a real person is a coincidence.

**Level:** mid, 4 years. **Target roles:** forward-deployed engineer, AI deployment engineer, solutions engineer (AI), customer engineer. **Background:** software engineer who spent 2 years deploying an AI platform inside large customers.

A forward-deployed engineer resume has to show two things at once: you write production code, and customers trust you in the room. Most FDE resumes lean too far toward one. The bullets below pair the technical work with the customer outcome.

## Why this works

- **Renewal is the headline.** "All 6 renewed and 4 expanded" is the number an FDE hiring manager most wants to see.
- **Technical depth is specific.** Named connectors and per-customer eval sets show she writes the code herself.
- **The best bullet is one customer story.** 22% to 67% straight-through processing at one insurer, with the mechanism (confidence threshold plus human review) and the business result.
- **Product feedback loop is measured.** 3 features shipped from field requests, now used by 11 other customers. This is what separates an FDE from a consultant.
- **The customer skills line** is unusual on an engineering resume and correct for this role.
- **Travel willingness is in the header.** FDE roles often require it; saying it up front saves a screening call.

## What to change for each kind of employer

**Frontier lab (FDE or applied AI roles at model companies).** Labs deploy their models with large customers and want engineers who can build the agent on site. Lead with the agent and eval work; add any prompt or fine-tuning work you did for a customer. Mention the scale of customer data handled.

**Startup.** Startups hire FDEs to land the first 10 big customers. Lead with time-to-production (9 weeks) and renewals. Add any pre-sales work (scoping calls, proofs of concept that converted). Breadth matters more than depth here.

**Enterprise (internal AI deployment teams, consultancies).** Lead with the model risk committee approval, security questionnaires and regulated-industry customers. Use the posting's terms: "solution architect", "AI adoption", "change management" where they fit.
