A technical AI PM resume is judged on two questions: did your product decisions move numbers, and do you understand the system well enough to make good trade-offs with engineers? The bullets should show both, and should name the quality bar you set.

## Why this works

- **Business outcomes lead.** Adoption (41%), upgrade survey share, and $2.4M ARR from a cost decision. PM resumes live or die on these.
- **The quality bar is his.** "Defined the launch bar" and "delayed the other 2 until they did" show the judgment AI PM interviews probe hardest: when to say not yet.
- **Technical fluency without pretending to be the engineer.** "Worked with engineering to cut cost per use by 64%" names the mechanisms and gives credit, which is how strong technical PMs write.
- **The failure review is a habit, not an event.** Weekly sampling of 200 outputs tells a reader this PM looks at real outputs.
- **The engineering background is short but present.** It explains the technical skills line without taking space from PM work.

## What to change for each kind of employer

**Frontier lab (product roles on model or API products).** Labs want PMs who understand model behavior and developer users. Lead with the API and platform growth numbers and the eval-based launch bar. Add any work with model evaluation or safety review.

**Startup.** Lead with 0-to-1 speed (spec to GA in 4 months) and revenue impact. Add anything you did hands-on: prototypes, SQL analysis, writing prompts. Early-stage startups often want a PM who can build a prototype themselves.

**Enterprise.** Lead with governance and adoption: the launch bar, the review process, and rollout across many customers. Add any work on admin controls, data permissions or compliance for AI features. Use the posting's terms (AI strategy, responsible AI, copilot) where true.
